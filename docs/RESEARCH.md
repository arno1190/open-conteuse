## Conclusions to test

Borrow Yoto's card interaction, Lunii's prepared story graphs, Merlin's curated library and Morphée's finite bedtime sessions. Prefer publisher RSS downloads over a dependency on Spotify playback. Cards map locally to packages or stations. Avoid hard-coded station addresses. No existing Merlin interoperability implementation was verified during this research.

The owner has a Lunii and Merlin at home. Identify generation and firmware before experimenting. Begin with UX observations and USB/network descriptors, then current measurements and optional teardown. No proprietary content or firmware is included here.
