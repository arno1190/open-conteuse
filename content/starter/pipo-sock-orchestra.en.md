## Recording direction / Direction de lecture

Warm, conversational storyteller. Distinct but restrained dialogue; no cartoon squeaks. Natural pace, short scene pauses and one optional reflection pause. Read only the script. No music or effects are supplied. Never imply a listener answer was heard. Full listening/editorial approval remains pending.

## Story / Histoire

Someone in the laundry basket kept saying, “Me first. Me first. Me first.”

Pipo lifted a towel. Underneath stood three socks. A red stripy one. A green spotty one. And a purple one with a little hole in its heel.

“We're giving a concert to find our friends,” explained Red. “We've all been separated. If they recognise our music, they'll come back.”

“Good idea!” said Pipo. “Can I help?”

Red immediately bounced onto the edge of the basket.

“I'll do the beginning! Boom! Boom! Boom!”

“No, me!” said Green. It tapped its toe twice. “Tap tap! Tap tap!”

They started together. Red bounced over Green's tapping. Green sped up to catch Red's bouncing. They sounded like two people trying to squeeze through the same door at once.

At the bottom of the basket, Purple made no sound at all.

Pipo raised a wooden spoon like a conductor.

“Everyone watch me. One, two, three!”

The two socks began again, louder than before. Red bounced so high it landed on top of the spoon. Green started laughing. The concert stopped before it had properly begun.

“Let's try quietly,” said Pipo.

They played quietly, but still at the same time. Their little sounds kept pushing each other out of the way. Now it was an argument in whispers.

Purple came forward.

“What about me?”

“What sound can you make?” asked Pipo.

Purple took a big breath. It wriggled, puffed itself up and shook its heel. Nothing happened.

“The air escapes through my hole. I'm no use.”

Purple looked at the door. “What if we listen?” Pipo put down the spoon. For a moment, nobody spoke. And in that moment, two little taps came from behind the door. Tap tap.

Green turned round.

“That's my sister! She couldn't hear us with all that noise!”

Pipo looked at Purple.

“Wait. You've just found what we were missing. An empty space, so someone else can answer.”

Pipo placed Red on the left, Green in the middle and Purple on the right.

“One at a time. Red: one big step. Green: two little ones. Purple: we wait.”

Boom. Tap tap. Then silence.

If you like, you can follow the little rhythm in your head. One big step, two little ones, and a space for an answer.

Behind the door came tap tap! Under the armchair came boom! A purple sock stirred on the washing line. Nobody had noticed it. It had been waiting for its turn too.

Pipo opened the door. Six socks met in the middle of the rug. The reds took one big step. The greens took two little ones. The purples left a lovely quiet space.

This time, the whole room seemed to know when to answer.

As they climbed back into the drawer, Red asked,

“Who's going first tomorrow?”

Purple smiled.

“First, we listen.”

## Away from the screen / Loin de l’écran

Choose a very gentle rhythm: two taps on your knees, then a silence. Somebody else can answer with a different rhythm. No small objects or loud sounds are needed. Playing the silence is part of the concert too.
