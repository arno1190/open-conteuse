## Recording direction / Direction de lecture

Warm, unhurried voice, 85–105 words/minute. Leave 2 seconds between paragraphs and 8 seconds after the listener invitation. Speak the story only: these directions are not narration. No sudden effects; music is optional and none is supplied. Child may listen without answering or moving.

## Story / Histoire

On washing day, Pipo found three socks without their partners. One had stripes, one had spots and one had a little hole in its heel.

“We want to play a concert,” announced the stripy sock.

“Very good,” said Pipo. “Which instruments do you play?”

The three socks looked at each other. They had absolutely no idea.

The spotty sock tried blowing. It went, “Fffff.”

The stripy sock shuffled across the rug. It went, “Shhh, shhh.”

The sock with the hole did nothing at all.

“I am waiting for my turn,” it explained.

Pipo thought for a moment. He put his hands on his knees and tapped gently. “Pom, pom.”

The spotty sock answered, “Fffff.” The stripy sock added, “Shhh, shhh.” Then the sock with the hole raised one finger to ask for silence.

Everybody stopped. In the quiet, they heard the clock going tick-tock.

“It is playing with us!” whispered Pipo.

If you feel like it, you can try a little rhythm on your knees. Very gently. Two taps, then a silence. Or you can imagine it without moving.

They practised again. Sometimes the socks started together. Sometimes they got muddled and began again, laughing. Pipo decided the concert would be short: just long enough for one made-up song.

Just then, three matching socks appeared at the edge of the washing basket. Their partners!

“You found us!” said the stripy sock.

“We followed the music,” they replied.

The orchestra now had six socks. That was rather a lot for a little rug.

So the three newcomers settled down in the audience. Pipo made a small bow. The concert began with two taps, a soft puff and a silence.

Then the audience applauded without a sound. Six little woolly feet waved in the air. For this very first concert, that was exactly right.

## Away from the screen / Loin de l’écran

Choose a very gentle rhythm: two taps on your knees, then a silence. Somebody else can answer with a different rhythm. No small objects or loud sounds are needed. Playing the silence is part of the concert too.
