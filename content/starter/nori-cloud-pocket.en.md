## Recording direction / Direction de lecture

Warm, unhurried voice, 85–105 words/minute. Leave 2 seconds between paragraphs and 8 seconds after the listener invitation. Speak the story only: these directions are not narration. No sudden effects; music is optional and none is supplied. Child may listen without answering or moving.

## Story / Histoire

Nori had a pocket for treasures. Inside it she kept a boat-shaped leaf, a purple button and a bus ticket. One morning, she found a very small cloud in there too.

“Are you lost?” she asked.

“A little. I was following the others. Then I stopped to watch an ant. Now I cannot see the sky.”

Nori opened her pocket wide. “The sky is right there.”

The cloud hesitated. “It is very big.”

Nori sat down on a bench. “We could start by looking at a little piece.”

They chose the square of sky between two branches. A bird crossed it. Then a leaf. Then nothing at all.

For a while, they listened. Nori heard a wheel rolling over gravel, a door closing and, far away, somebody calling a dog.

If you feel like it, listen around you for a few moments. Which sound reaches your ears? Or you can stay with Nori inside the story.

“Perhaps I am a cloud who prefers little walks,” said the cloud.

“Sometimes I do too,” said Nori.

She showed the cloud her purple button. The cloud tried to copy its shape. First it became round, then wonderfully wonky. Nori laughed.

Next it tried to copy the boat-shaped leaf. This time, it made a perfect sail, but forgot the boat underneath.

“It is a boat for the sky!” said Nori.

The cloud floated up to the back of the bench. From there, it recognised a large cloud shaped like a teapot.

“My aunt! She is waiting for me!”

It floated away, taking its time. Nori waved.

In her pocket, she still had the button, the leaf and the ticket. She had not brought home a new treasure. She had taken a little walk with one of them. And that had been quite an adventure.

## Away from the screen / Loin de l’écran

From somewhere comfortable and safe, find three shapes in the clouds or in the pattern of a piece of fabric. You can also draw your own clouds. No need to go outside: a drawing can make the journey too.
