## Direct-sales model, not a quote

| Planning item | Galia Mini | Galia Explore |
|---|---:|---:|
| Target retail incl. 20% VAT | €99.00 | €129.00 |
| Net retail before VAT | €82.50 | €107.50 |
| Illustrative maximum landed, tested unit COGS target | €35.00 | €45.00 |
| Gross difference before other business costs | €47.50 | €62.50 |
| Gross difference / net retail | 57.6% | 58.1% |

COGS targets are **not achieved BOM totals or supplier offers**. They must include electronics, battery, housing, assembly, test, packaging, inbound freight and duties at an agreed production quantity and yield. The gross difference must also pay for product development, compliance, content and narration, VAT administration, payment costs, fulfilment, outbound delivery, returns, warranty, support and marketing. Prototype quantities cannot be assumed to meet production COGS. Engineering/NRE and certification need separate budgets.

Retail/wholesale distribution requires another margin model; do not apply the direct-sales targets to reseller economics. Do not announce a final selling price until written manufacturing quotes and the full business model support it. The website has no order, reservation or checkout mechanism.

## Editorial direction

Mallow informed a warm, child-led invitation and tangible play ritual. Merlin informed a clear adult library story. Lunii informed imaginative listening and activities that continue off screen. Our words, stories, characters, artwork, colours and mark are original. No competitor testimonials, claimed benefits, catalogue rights, logos, photographs or voice-interaction functionality are reused. “Galia” remains a working identity pending naming clearance.

Third-party AI/TTS subscription charges, API usage and future integration work are not included in the retail objectives. A consumer subscription must not be assumed to provide API credits or audio-export rights. No provider is connected or billed by this showcase.
